# SkyHedge Context

SkyHedge is a Solana-backed climate protection product. This glossary keeps product, protocol, and demo language consistent as the project moves from research catalog to Devnet proof.

## Language

**Rainfall protection**:
A fixed-payout contract that pays when a pinned rainfall index meets its published trigger.
_Avoid_: Option, future, trade, bet

**Agricultural index**:
A release-gated rainfall reference area tied to a crop region, precise location label, observation window, and settlement evidence package.
_Avoid_: City market, commodity market, trading pair

**Reference location**:
The city or area used to describe an agricultural index, shown as locality, administrative area, and country.
_Avoid_: Coverage area, insured boundary

**Catalog region**:
A broad browsing group such as North America, South America, or Emerging markets.
_Avoid_: Settlement location, coverage region

**Protected amount**:
The customer-facing payout amount if the rainfall trigger is met.
_Avoid_: Position size, notional, leverage

**Preview premium**:
A non-binding USD estimate shown before live USDC collateral and checkout are available.
_Avoid_: Price, token cost, execution price

**Settlement evidence**:
The final NOAA observation, normalized rainfall value, methodology hash, source hash, and timing proof used to resolve an index.
_Avoid_: Weather feed, oracle guess, generated observation

**Data unavailable**:
A deterministic outcome used when NOAA cannot provide valid final data within the source deadline.
_Avoid_: Manual override, fallback data, admin settlement

**Builder mode**:
The Solana-facing demonstration surface for grants, reviewers, and ecosystem partners.
_Avoid_: Customer mode, trading terminal

**Customer mode**:
The plain-language product surface for non-crypto buyers such as farm cooperatives.
_Avoid_: DeFi app, wallet-first flow

**SKYT**:
The six-decimal Devnet-only test collateral used to exercise the protocol before USDC.
_Avoid_: Customer token, investment asset, production currency
