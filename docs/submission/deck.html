Money flow

The payout is already in the vault.

LP collateral + buyer premium → vault

Each city market has a PDA-owned vault. A buy is rejected unless it covers every reserved payout, including the new purchase.

Vault → buyer / protocol / LPs

Trigger hit: buyer collects the fixed payout. At close: 1% of cover goes to the protocol; LPs redeem the remainder pro rata.

Premium = NOAA probability × 1.15, rounded up, plus 1% of cover. The 15% margin stays for LPs. Example: 20% chance, 100 SKYT cover → pay 24; 23 stays for LPs if not triggered, 1 is the protocol fee at close.

No trigger: LPs keep premiums after fees. Data-unavailable: full premium refund, fee waived. Payouts come from LP collateral; SkyHedge earns the fee.

SkyHedge5 / 10

Settlement · trust today

Public data. On-chain rules. One trusted key.

What SkyHedge posts

After the window ends, the worker reads NOAA GHCN-Daily, rejects missing or quality-flagged days, and posts the total plus a SHA-256 hash of the records.

What the program checks

Only the settlement authority may post. Station, methodology and exact window must match. Anyone can then settle: total versus fixed trigger.

No valid reading by window end + 7 days → anyone can mark data-unavailable → buyers reclaim their full premium, including the fee.

Trusted today: SkyHedge’s key copies NOAA faithfully. The hash supports audit; it does not prove the number is correct. A decentralized oracle is next, and is not built yet.
