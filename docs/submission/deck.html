Settlement

How settlement works

  • Observation window closes → settlement authority posts hashed NOAA package.
  • Program compares cumulative rain to the immutable trigger.
  • Triggered → buyer claims fixed payout from the vault. Not triggered → premiums stay for LPs.
  • 1% protocol fee accrues in the premium; waived on data-unavailable refunds.
SkyHedge5 / 10

Oracle failures

Fail closed. Refund when data never arrives.

  • Incomplete or flagged NOAA days are rejected — never invented.
  • After data deadline with no observation: permissionless mark_data_unavailable → premium refund.
  • Once an observation exists: permissionless settle_market so settlement cannot stall.
